A few straight answers

Before you
take the next step.

Do I need a competitor's quote to get started?+

No. Anyone can request a free assessment. The 5% offer applies only when you have a qualifying written estimate from a registered Vermont contractor. Without one, you still get a free site visit and a detailed estimate.

Is an assessment a commitment to buy?+

No. The site visit and the estimate are free. You review the plan and decide whether to move forward. There is no obligation.

What if I only need a repair?+

Tell us. We do roof repairs too. The 5% offer covers full roof replacements only, and if the assessment shows a repair is the right call, we'll say so rather than sell you a new roof.

What does the shingle warranty cover?+

Our 10-year workmanship warranty covers asphalt shingle installations. It covers leaks or failures caused by how the roof was installed. Material defects fall under the manufacturer's warranty, and storm damage falls under homeowner's insurance.

What happens after I request an assessment?+

We reach out within 1 business day to schedule your free site visit. At the visit we look at your roof, your attic ventilation, and anything else that affects the job. Then you get a written estimate with scope, materials, and price, and the decision is yours.
