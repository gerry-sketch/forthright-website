The details you never see decide how long it lasts.

Is an assessment a commitment to buy?+

No. The site visit and the proposal are free. You review the plan and decide whether to move forward. There is no obligation.

Do you have to remove my old siding?+

On homes over 15 years old, we almost always recommend full tear-off. It lets us inspect the sheathing, replace damaged sections, and install new house wrap and flashing properly. We assess each house and tell you what we find.

James Hardie or LP SmartSide?+

Both perform well here. James Hardie is our default for most homes. LP SmartSide is a strong choice if you prefer the look and workability of wood. We carry both and can show you samples.

What does the workmanship warranty cover?+

Our 2-year workmanship warranty covers failures caused by how the siding was installed. Material defects fall under the manufacturer's warranty, which we register in your name at closeout. Storm damage falls under homeowner's insurance.

What happens after I request an assessment?+

We reach out within 1 business day to schedule your free site visit. Then you get a written proposal with scope, materials, and price, and the decision is yours.
