About Forthright

Accountable By Design

Forthright was built on a simple idea - be accountable to the customer, to our suppliers, to our subcontractors, and to each other. That commitment is the foundation.

Our Story

Why We Started Forthright

Forthright was founded by someone who came to construction from the outside - with a background in marketing, sales, and business operations across SaaS and commodity markets, and as COO of a construction company.

That perspective shapes how we run jobs. We communicate like a business, not a trade shop. Estimates are documented. Timelines are tracked. Clients don't have to chase us down for updates.

We've built a team with real trade expertise to back it up. The combination - business discipline and skilled field execution - is what Forthright is built on.

The Team

Who You'll Be Working With

JL
Jason LeBlanc Director of Preconstruction

Jason leads preconstruction at Forthright - estimating, project planning, and business development across residential and commercial work. Two decades spanning the trades, business ownership, and operations inform how he approaches budgeting, value engineering, constructability reviews, and building envelope systems.

He works with general contractors, homeowners, architects, developers, and property managers to set realistic budgets and surface practical solutions before construction starts. His view: the strongest projects are built on preparation, teamwork, and straight communication.

CG
Carl Grumbine Director of Sales and Marketing

Carl leads sales and marketing at Forthright, working primarily with homeowners and property management companies to match them to the right solution for the job.

A Saint Michael's College business graduate with three-plus years in the industry, he is known for responsiveness and for understanding what a client actually needs before recommending a path. He makes starting a project feel straightforward rather than overwhelming - answering questions, setting expectations, and making sure nobody is left in the dark.
